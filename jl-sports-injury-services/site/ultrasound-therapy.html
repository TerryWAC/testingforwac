Ultrasound Therapy

Ultrasound therapy is used to aid soft tissue repair and to reduce scar tissue following injury.

45 minutes From £38 Gosforth, Newcastle

How it works

Sound waves are emitted through the soft tissue, causing micro-vibrations with a thermal effect. That increases blood flow to the area, accelerates healing and helps remodel scar tissue so it lays down in a more functional pattern.

Where it fits

It is most valuable in the middle stages of healing — once the acute phase has settled and the priority becomes getting good quality tissue laid down before you load it properly.

Comfortable and passive

Treatment is painless. You will feel a gentle warmth and the movement of the applicator head, and nothing more.

Is this right for you?

Ultrasound Therapy is commonly the right choice if any of the following sound familiar:

  • Soft tissue injuries in the healing phase
  • Scar tissue from an older injury
  • Tendon and ligament repair
  • Areas that need blood flow before loading

If you are not sure, book an injury assessment instead — it identifies exactly what you need and includes treatment in the same appointment.
