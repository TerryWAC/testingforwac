Conditions we treat

If it hurts when you move, it is worth getting looked at.

Most people arrive with one of these. Whatever it is, the assessment finds the cause before anything gets treated.

Knee pain

Runner's knee, patellar tendinopathy, ligament and cartilage injuries, and knees that ache after loading.

Back pain

Lower back pain from lifting, sitting or sport — assessed properly so you know whether it is muscular, joint or nerve related.

Neck pain

Stiffness and referred pain from desk posture, training or sleeping awkwardly — including headaches that start in the neck.

Sciatica & nerve pain

Sciatica, nerve irritation and the pins, numbness or burning that travels down an arm or leg.

Muscle strains & tightness

Strains, tears, chronic tightness and trigger points through the hamstrings, calves, quads, back and shoulders.

Shoulder pain

Rotator cuff irritation, impingement, pressing pain and shoulders that have lost range from overhead work or desk posture.

Wrist & elbow pain

Tennis and golfer's elbow, tendon irritation, repetitive strain and grip pain from lifting, racket sport, climbing or keyboard work.

How it works

Four steps from injured to back in training.

01

Book in two minutes

Pick a treatment and a time that works. Instant confirmation, no waiting for a callback, no deposit.

02

Get assessed properly

A full hands-on assessment finds the cause, not just the sore bit. You get a clear diagnosis and a realistic timescale.

03

Treat and rehabilitate

Hands-on treatment plus loaded, coached rehab on the gym floor — so the fix holds when you go back to training.

04

Back to full training

We re-test as you go and discharge you when you are ready. The aim is to get you out the door, not to keep you coming back.
