How it works

Four steps from injured to back in training.

01

Book in two minutes

Pick a treatment and a time that works. Instant confirmation, no waiting for a callback, no deposit.

02

Get assessed properly

A full hands-on assessment finds the cause, not just the sore bit. You get a clear diagnosis and a realistic timescale.

03

Treat and rehabilitate

Hands-on treatment plus loaded, coached rehab on the gym floor — so the fix holds when you go back to training.

04

Back to full training

We re-test as you go and discharge you when you are ready. The aim is to get you out the door, not to keep you coming back.
