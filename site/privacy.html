Privacy notice

How your personal information is collected, used and protected.

Last updated: [DATE]

Who is responsible for your data

[FIRM LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us") is the data controller for the personal information described in this notice. You can contact us using the details on our contact page.

What we collect

To give mortgage or protection advice we normally need:

  • Identity and contact details — name, date of birth, address history, phone, email
  • Financial information — income, employment, outgoings, existing credit, bank statements
  • Property details — the property you own or intend to buy
  • Credit history, obtained from credit reference agencies with your permission
  • Health and lifestyle information, where you ask us to advise on protection products
  • How you found the site — campaign tags in the link you followed, and the referring address

Health information is "special category" data under UK GDPR. We only collect it where it is necessary to advise on or arrange insurance, and we rely on the substantial public interest condition for insurance purposes in Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To provide advice and arrange products you have asked for — performance of a contract, or steps taken at your request before entering one.
  • To meet our regulatory obligations — legal obligation. This includes FCA record-keeping, anti-money-laundering checks and identity verification.
  • To contact you about your enquiry — legitimate interests, or your consent where you have submitted the website form.
  • To remind you when your mortgage rate is ending — legitimate interests. You can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

Only where it is needed to do the job you have asked of us:

  • Lenders and insurers, when submitting an application on your behalf
  • Credit reference and fraud prevention agencies
  • Our network or principal firm, where we operate as an appointed representative
  • The software we use to run the business — CRM, email and mortgage sourcing tools — acting as data processors under contract
  • Regulators, and other bodies where we are required by law to disclose

We never sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

Mortgage advice records are kept for at least six years from the end of our relationship, as our regulatory obligations require. Records relating to protection and other long-term products are kept for longer. We do not keep personal data for longer than we need it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Ask for data to be erased, where we have no continuing legal reason to keep it
  • Object to, or ask us to restrict, certain processing
  • Ask for your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis we rely on

To exercise any of these, get in touch using the details on our contact page. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies and browser storage

This website sets no tracking or advertising cookies. It uses your browser's own storage for two things, both of which stay on your device and are removed when you clear your browser data:

  • Theme preference — whether you chose the light or dark view. Kept until you clear it.
  • Enquiry context — if you arrived from a link carrying campaign tags (utm_source and similar), those and the referring address are held for the duration of your visit so that an enquiry you send tells us which advert or link brought you here. Cleared when you close the tab.

Neither is used to build a profile of you or to follow you across other websites.

What the enquiry form records

Alongside the answers you give, an enquiry sends the campaign tags and referring address described above, and — if you used one of the calculators before getting in touch — the figures you entered into it. This is so the first conversation can start from where you already got to, rather than repeating it.

If you sign up for a reminder about your rate ending, we hold your email address and the month you gave us for that purpose, until you ask us to stop or the reminder is no longer relevant.
